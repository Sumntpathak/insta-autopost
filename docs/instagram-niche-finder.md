# Instagram niche finder

## First hypothesis, 13 November 2026

ShipStack can test 15-second consumer-tech demos under **Deal or Hype?** Start with Black Friday tech price checks, then use the same format for phone features and everyday AI tools. Black Friday is a timely topic; Instagram's actual topic momentum still needs to be measured. Current Reels reporting gives us a format hypothesis: show a specific problem, the process and a visible result.

- [Amazon's November Black Friday calendar and price-tracking tools](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/retail/when-is-black-friday-faq)
- [Google's 2026 holiday shopping guide](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/shopping/holiday-shopping-when-to-buy-guide/)
- [Public Reels trend sample, October 31 to November 7](https://lightreel.ai/blogs/whats-trending-on-instagram)

## Collect daily

Start with three lanes: tech deal checks, useful AI demos, and satisfying visual puzzles as the account's comparison group. Sample recent public Reels from a rotating watchlist of creators across account sizes. Ingest links and visible counts from Instagram's in-app search and trends surfaces or a permitted data provider. Keep a manual submission option. Use news and search signals to explain timing. Treat missing engagement counts as missing.

Record Reel URL, creator, time posted, time observed, region where known, topic, format, visible views and interactions, and source. Deduplicate by Reel URL and sample again after 24 hours to estimate momentum. Compare each Reel to the median for the creator's recent posts at similar ages when that baseline exists.

## Rank and output

Start with a 100-point rubric: 35 for view velocity relative to creator baseline, 25 for traction across independent creators, 15 for freshness, 15 for a repeatable original series, and 10 for fit with ShipStack. Require examples from three independent creators before labeling a topic Instagram-validated. Mark thin samples as hypotheses and show confidence separately. The daily output is a ranked top three with source links, geography, observed metrics, a 15-second hook and a shot list. The workflow should output briefs for review.

## Measure

Start with three original clips per lane. If the account has [Trial Reels](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/12/trial-reels-try-content-non-followers-first-see-what-perfoms-best/), test there with automatic sharing off. Use comparable posting windows and read at 24 and 72 hours. Rank by follows per 1,000 accounts reached, then shares and saves per 1,000 reached, with watch time as a diagnostic. If per-Reel follows are unavailable, report the gap and use shares and saves as provisional signals. Compare country breakdowns where Insights provides them.

First clip: **Would you buy these headphones at 40% off?** Show the offer at 0–2 seconds, show the dated price history for the same SKU and market at 2–10 seconds, then show the verdict and next item at 10–15 seconds. Verify the live offer and price-history source before recording.

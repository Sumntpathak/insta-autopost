# Recorded sounds used in The Last Laugh

## Laughter — laugh.wav

- Original file: `wahaha.wav` by code4fukui.
- [Pinned GitHub source](https://github.com/code4fukui/sound-cc0/blob/0bdbbe370c42897e12b7c5b0b26d96228e0d2931/wahaha.wav).
- [File-specific license record](https://github.com/code4fukui/sound-cc0/blob/0bdbbe370c42897e12b7c5b0b26d96228e0d2931/sounds.csv) lists the recording as CC0, recorded with a MacBook Pro microphone on February 10, 2024.
- The repository's generic LICENSE is the Unlicense; the sound catalog specifically marks this sound CC0. Both are public-domain-style dedications.
- Changes in the mix: trim leading/trailing silence, normalize, add short fades, adjust playback rate to distinguish the two characters, repeat and pan to match their positions.

## Water drop — water-drop.wav

- [Pinned GitHub source](https://github.com/Lelio88/dewdrop/blob/35da4d0ec47d27bf582c7aa7102c40a278eff49b/android/app/src/main/res/raw/drop.wav).
- [File-specific credits and license](https://github.com/Lelio88/dewdrop/blob/35da4d0ec47d27bf582c7aa7102c40a278eff49b/CREDITS.md).
- The repository attributes it to `Water_drop_9.wav`, [Freesound #166325](https://freesound.org/s/166325/), under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
- The repository documents its own processing: trimming, fades, normalization and mono conversion. The source WAV is preserved here as downloaded.
- Changes in the film mix: silence trim, normalization, short fades, cue placement and stereo panning.

Attribution is not required by the stated CC0 licenses. Source credit is retained for provenance. `sources.json` records pinned commits, source URLs and SHA-256 checksums. Other grunts, weather, spring, splash and cup effects remain originally synthesized cartoon foley; no claim is made that every sound is a live recording.
